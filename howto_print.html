GAME 1 · 30 SECONDSAnimal Peekaboo

An animal sings a little tune. Listen — then play it back on the CASIO keyboard!

  1. Type a nickname, choose a level, tap ▶ Start.
  2. An animal peeks out and sings. Just listen — the clock stops while it sings.
  3. “Your turn!” Play the same notes, in the same order.
  4. Right → next tune. Wrong → you see the answer and get a new tune.
  5. Clear as many tunes as you can in 30 seconds!

GAME 2 · 1 MINUTEPiano Challenge

Read the score and play a whole song before the time runs out!

  1. Type a nickname, choose a level and a song, tap ▶ Start.
  2. The blue note on the score is the one to play now.
  3. Beginner / Intermediate: the next key lights up.
  4. A chord = press all its notes together (any order).
  5. Finish the song in 1 minute. A wrong key is a miss — keep going!
